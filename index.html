Zoyra

a magazine of feminist politics and culture

Zoyra is an independent and deeply feminist magazine. We understand feminism as an imperative response to a world under threat of collapse, and a salve to the wounds of oppression. We’re interested in asking meaningful questions about power, choice, capital, technology, culture, labor, pleasure, and our shared futures. We are intentionally low-tech, anti-capitalist, and accessible in practice.

We are seeking curious and thoughtful essays, stories, pitches for regular columns, and feminist book reviews for our first issue! To send us a pitch or idea, you can do so on our Subfolio or send us an email. Our inaugural issue's theme is Feminist Ways of Being. Intersectional feminist practice is a strategy while also being a salve to the wounds inflicted from centuries of patriarchal impression across the temporal landscape. We ask: what can feminism do for us today, right now? How can it help us heal, relate, play, and understand ourselves and each other better?  Specific topics we would be excited about stories on feminist puppetry, urban agriculture, smartphones and internet use, pornography and sex work, existential crisis of being a feminist under capitalism, feminist sheepherders, and the feminist praxis of not being a flaky friend.

Zoyra is an independent effort. If you have an idea for somewhere we should stock the magazine or would like to submit, email hello@zoyramag.com.

Zoyra's low tech ethos:

Zoyra is intentionally minimal, slow, and leans away from a digital presence.

This webpage is static, built with plain HTML and CSS. This helps to lessen energy and environmental impacts. This also results in increased accessibility for people with disabilities, and is a reflection of our effort to balance ethics, visual appeal, and function.

Keep in touch! We keep our presence online very small, you won’t find us on social media. Write us a letter or email.
