MAGAZINE NAME

An independent print publication on contemporary feminism from the Pacific Northwest.

MAGAZINE NAME publishes writing that approaches the world through a feminist lens. We welcome creative nonfiction, field reports, criticism, media reviews, opinion, interviews, visual work, and forms that resist easy categorization. Whether you're writing about a local trail, a city council meeting, a film, a friendship, or a question you can't quite shake, we're interested in work that is curious, thoughtful, and engaged with the world around it.

To submit a pitch or idea, email hello@magazinename.com.

MAGAZINE NAME is an independent effort. If you have an idea for somewhere we should stock the magazine, would like to collaborate, or simply want to say hello, we'd love to hear from you.

About this website

This website is intentionally small.

It's a static webpage built with plain HTML and CSS—no tracking, no AI-generated content, and no unnecessary scripts. Keeping it simple means it loads quickly, uses very little energy, works well on older devices and slower internet connections, and can be maintained for years with minimal resources. We believe publishing should be thoughtful not only in what it prints, but also in how it exists online.
