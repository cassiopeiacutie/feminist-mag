Zoyra

a magazine of feminist politics and culture

Zoyra is an independent and deeply feminist magazine. We understand feminism as an imperative response to a world under threat of collapse, and a salve to the wounds of oppression. We’re interested in asking meaningful questions about power, choice, capital, technology, culture, labor, pleasure, and our shared futures. We are intentionally low-tech, anti-capitalist, and accessible in practice.

We are seeking curious and thoughtful essays, stories, and media reviews for our first issue! To send us a pitch or idea, you can do so on our Subfolio or send us an email. Our inaugural issue's theme is what can feminism do for us right now, in this moment? Where is it needed most right now?

Zoyra is an independent effort. If you have an idea for somewhere we should stock the magazine, would like to collaborate, or simply want to say hello, email hello@zoyramag.com

Zoyra's low tech ethos:

Zoyra is intentionally minimal, slow, and leans away from a digital presence.

This webpage is static, built with plain HTML and CSS. This helps to lessen energy and environmental impacts, as thus less dependency on data centers. This also results in increased accessibility for people with disabilities, and is a reflection of our goal to balance ethics, visual appeal, and function.

We keep our presence online very small. Thus, you won’t find us on social media.
