The Feminist Magazine

The Feminist is an independent magazine for long-form discussion on how patriarchal culture and politics shapes our lives. We understand feminism as an imperative response to a world under threat of collapse, and a salve to the wounds of oppression. We’re interested in asking meaningful questions about power, choice, capital, care, community, culture, labor, pleasure, and what comes next. We’re interested in pieces that are insightful and curious, without being overly-academic in spirit.

To submit a pitch or idea, visit on our Subfolio. Our inaugural issue's theme is what can feminism do for us todal? How can it help us heal, relate, play, and understand ourselves and the world around us? Send us your ideas, essays, criticism or media reviews, reportage, interview, poetry, experimental work or general ideas for collaboration.

The Feminist is an independent effort. If you have an idea for somewhere we should stock the magazine, would like to collaborate, or simply want to say hello, we'd love to hear from you.

About this website's low tech ethos:

This website is intentionally simple.

It's a static webpage built with plain HTML and CSS—no tracking, no AI-generated content, and no unnecessary scripts. Keeping it simple means it loads quickly, uses very little energy, works well on older devices and slower internet connections, and can be maintained for years with minimal resources. We believe publishing should be thoughtful not only in what it prints, but also in how it exists online.
