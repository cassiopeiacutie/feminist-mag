Zoyra Magazine

a magazine of feminist politics and culture

Zoyra is an independent magazine for long-form discussion on how patriarchal culture and politics shapes our lives. We understand feminism as an imperative response to a world under threat of collapse, and a salve to the wounds of oppression. We’re interested in asking meaningful questions about power, choice, capital, care, community, culture, labor, pleasure, and what comes next. We’re interested in pieces that are insightful and curious, without being overly-academic in spirit.

To submit a pitch or idea, visit on our Subfolio. Our inaugural issue's theme is what can feminism do for us todal? How can it help us heal, relate, play, and understand ourselves and the world around us? Send us your ideas, essays, criticism or media reviews, reportage, interview, poetry, experimental work or general ideas for collaboration.

Zoyra is an independent effort. If you have an idea for somewhere we should stock the magazine, would like to collaborate, or simply want to say hello, we'd love to hear from you.

About Zoyra's low tech ethos:

Zoyra's editorial team chooses to spend less time on screens. In the modern publishing world, this is a difficult feat. To lessen energy and environmental impacts and lessening dependency on data centers, the magazine and this website is intentionally simple. We attempt to balance ethical practices with beauty and function in all we create.

As a result, this website is a static webpage built with plain HTML and CSS. This also means more privacy and no tracking, and no unnecessary bloat. Keeping this website simple means it loads quickly, uses very little energy, works well on older devices, for people with disabilities, and slower internet connections, and can be maintained with less resources than the average webpage. Aiming to be low-tech, we keep our presence online very small.
